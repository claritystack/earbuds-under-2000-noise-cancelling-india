ANC vs ENC: What Is The Difference?

This distinction matters more in the best ANC earbuds under 2000 segment than anywhere else, because most products in this range use ENC and market it aggressively as noise cancelling. Here is the practical difference.

True ANC works by detecting external sound through built-in microphones and producing an opposite audio signal that cancels it before it reaches your ears. When you are sitting on a metro or working in a busy office, the background hum is actively reduced. You notice it when you turn ANC on and off — the difference is audible.

ENC works differently. It is designed for call clarity. The microphones pick up your voice and use processing to filter out the background noise so that the person on the other end of your call hears you more clearly. This is useful, but it does not affect what you hear at all. If you are trying to concentrate while commuting or studying, ENC alone will not help.

Passive isolation is a third factor that is often overlooked. The physical seal created by the ear tips when you insert them properly blocks a meaningful amount of external sound without any electronics involved. A well-fitting pair of earbuds with good ear tips can reduce ambient noise significantly on its own. All three models in this guide offer decent passive isolation, but this varies with ear fit, which is individual.

Is 100% noise cancellation possible?

No — not with any technology currently available in consumer earbuds. Even the most expensive ANC earbuds in the world do not eliminate all external sound. What ANC does is reduce consistent, low-frequency ambient noise like engine hum, crowd noise, and fan sounds. Sudden or sharp sounds like voices, honks, or announcements will still come through to some degree. At the best ANC earbuds under 2000 price point, you can expect meaningful reduction of background noise rather than complete silence — which is still a noticeable and practical improvement for commuting or focused work.

Which is better, ANC or ENC?

They serve different purposes, so the better one depends entirely on how you use your earbuds. ANC reduces the noise around you while you listen to music or content — useful for commuting, studying, or working in noisy environments. ENC improves how your voice sounds to the person you are calling — useful during phone calls, video meetings, and online classes. If you want the best ANC earbuds under 2000 for blocking out the world while you listen, ANC is what you need. If you are primarily on calls, ENC matters more.
