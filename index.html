ANC vs ENC: What's the Difference?

Before diving into reviews, it's worth understanding what separates real ANC from the marketing fluff — because in this price range, the distinction matters a lot.

How noise cancellation actually works at ₹2000

✅
True ANC — blocks noise while you listen

Microphones detect ambient sound and generate an opposing signal to cancel it. You actually hear less of the world around you during music playback. OnePlus Nord Buds 3 and boAt Nirvana Ion ANC both do this.

⚠️
ENC — only cleans up your voice on calls

ENC makes your voice clearer to the person you're calling. It does not reduce the noise you hear yourself. boAt Airdopes 300 uses ENC, not ANC — which is fine if calls are your priority.

💡
Passive Isolation — the earplug effect

The physical seal created by ear tips blocks some noise without any electronics. All three earbuds here offer decent passive isolation from their in-ear design.
